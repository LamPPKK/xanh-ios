Use GitHub Issues for reproducible browser, sync, accessibility, and content-blocking problems.

Open a support issue on GitHub

Include

  • Xanh version and build number.
  • iPhone or iPad model and iOS/iPadOS version.
  • Steps that reproduce the issue and what you expected.
  • Whether the problem occurs in a regular or private space.

Do not include

Do not post passwords, authentication codes, private URLs, cookies, personal CloudKit records, or screenshots containing sensitive page content.

Account and data deletion

Xanh has no Xanh account. Delete a profile from Settings to remove its local tabs, archived-tab metadata, bookmarks, history, Keychain lock, and WebKit website data. iCloud metadata deletion may take time to reach other offline devices.
